## Brand & Style

This design system blends Swiss modernist precision, editorial typography, and high-performance product software execution. Designed for discerning knowledge workers, operators, and executive leaders, the aesthetic rejects trendy dark glassmorphism, artificial neon glows, and novelty micro-interactions in favor of absolute clarity, typographic hierarchy, and meticulous spatial discipline.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modernist Discipline:** Crisp structural alignment, intentional negative space, and clear content prioritization inspired by classical editorial layouts and premium productivity software.
- **Human Tactility:** High-contrast slate typography paired with pure light grounds, balanced by subtle surface shifts rather than heavy ornamental elevations.
- **Enterprise Restraint:** Color is deployed with strict economy. Slate anchors the information architecture, while deep ocean tones signal intent and high-value actions without sensory fatigue.

## Colors

The palette is strictly calibrated for maximum legibility, visual calmness, and intentional focus on light canvases.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** Pure white (`#ffffff`) serves as the root substrate, maintaining clean contrast and radiant clarity.
- **Surfaces & Containers:** Neutral cool tints—Subtle Base (`#f8fafc`), Muted Surface (`#f1f5f9`), and Border Stroke (`#e2e8f0`)—provide separation without visual bulk.
- **Typography & Ink:** Deep graphite slate (`#0f172a`) delivers authoritative readability for primary content and headlines. Secondary slate (`#334155`) grounds descriptions and structured metadata, while muted slate (`#64748b`) handles captions, hints, and subtle dividers.
- **Brand Accents:** Deep Ocean (`#146c94`) commands primary actions, focused selections, and critical visual hooks. Sky Sapphire (`#0284c7`) acts as the interactive highlight for links, active tabs, and inline states. Deep Pine (`#0f766e`) is reserved for positive validation and verified badges.

## Typography

The typographical pairing pairs an authoritative editorial serif (`Newsreader`) with a contemporary, razor-sharp grotesque (`Hanken Grotesk`). This duality conveys human warmth and journalistic clarity for sectioning and key insights, supported by rigorous, utilitarian execution for dense data rows, interfaces, and controls.

### Implementation Guidelines
- **Editorial Headlines (`Newsreader`):** Applied to major landmarks, page hero sections, key narrative cards, and section dividers. It brings literary dignity to business workflows.
- **Product Hierarchy (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Applied systematically across subheaders, body reading text, tables, metrics, navigation, and controls. Optical kerning and tighter tracking apply to sizes 20px and above.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Tabular figures are mandatory for numerical data displays, balances, and operational ledgers to ensure crisp vertical alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

A mathematical 8pt grid establishes structural rhythm across all layouts. Structural boundaries rely on column discipline and clear padding hierarchies rather than heavy visual dividers.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout, max-width 1360px centered, with `margin: 2.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, fluid margins (`margin: 2rem`), and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, fluid edges with strict `margin-sm: 1rem` and `gutter-sm: 1rem`.

### Spatial Flow
Whitespace is treated as an active framing element. Cards and modules utilize balanced inner padding (`space-lg` desktop, `space-md` mobile) with content groupings tied together using compact spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is produced through surface tones and crisp borders rather than dense, blurred shadows.

### Border Precision
All panels, cards, toolbars, and inputs share a uniform `1px solid #e2e8f0` stroke. On nested containers, boundaries transition to background value shifts (e.g., `#ffffff` resting over `#f8fafc`) to minimize visual noise.

### Shadow Scale
- **Flat (Level 0):** `none` — default for canvas panels, embedded sections, and standard cards.
- **Subtle Rest (Level 1):** `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` — used on standalone cards and unselected interactive elements to create clean separation from the ground.
- **Hover / Interactive (Level 2):** `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` — applied on interactive card states and dropdown menus.
- **Floating Overlays (Level 3):** `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` — strictly reserved for modals, command palettes, and contextual popovers.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by tailored, compact radii (`roundedness: 1`). Elements have a sharp, architectural character, avoiding bulbous or pill-shaped contours.

### Corner Radii
- **Standard Controls & Elements (`rounded`):** `0.25rem` (4px). Standard buttons, text fields, badges, segmented controls, and table rows.
- **Cards & Floating Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px). Structural cards, modals, dropdown containers, and sheet overlays.
- **Outermost Shell Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px). Top-level app framing and expansive dashboard sections.

- **Focus:** Border `1px solid #146c94`, box-shadow `0 0 0 1px #146c94` (no wide, diffuse outer glows).
- **Error:** Border `1px solid #e11d48`, box-shadow `0 0 0 1px #e11d48`.

- Size 16px × 16px. Border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, radius 3px (checkbox) or circle (radio).
- **Checked:** Background `#146c94`, border `#146c94`, inner check icon crisp white. Focus ring strictly matching the accent outline.

- Inner card headers utilize bottom border `1px solid #f1f5f9`, standard padding `16px 20px`. Editorial narrative cards combine a subtle top accent bar or `Newsreader` display headlines.

### Lists & Data Rows
- Border-bottom `1px solid #f1f5f9`. Row hover state transitions background to `#f8fafc`.
- Key numerical and identifier columns utilize tabular numbers with monospaced alignment properties.
